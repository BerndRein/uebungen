# Brief: Obsidian 30s spot

## Intent

A 30-second, music-led brand spot that feels as if Obsidian's own team made it.
It must prove two things at once: world-class motion design and a pitch-perfect
match to the Obsidian brand (obsidian.md, brand page, real product UI).

The story is a transformation: thoughts scatter → you connect them → patterns
appear → you think in space → it is all yours → shaped to your mind →
"Sharpen your thinking."

## Decisions (autonomous run; no questions asked)

- **Sound: music + designed SFX, no voice-over.** Obsidian has never cut a
  narrated ad; its own launch media (canvas hero loop, feature loops) is
  silent, calm, real-UI motion. Its voice is short declarative headlines
  ending in a period. Text supers carry the message, music carries the emotion.
- **Copy reuses Obsidian's own language** wherever possible: "Your thoughts
  are yours.", "Your mind is unique.", "Free without limits.", "Sharpen your
  thinking." (hero), "Get Obsidian" (CTA).
- **Features chosen by user pull:** Links/backlinks, Graph view, Canvas,
  local-first plain-text ownership, plugins/themes. Sync/Publish left out
  (paid add-ons, would dilute 30s).
- **End:** official gradient crystal mark + wordmark lockup (unaltered),
  hero line "Sharpen your thinking.", "Free without limits.", purple
  "Get Obsidian" button, obsidian.md.

## Customizations

- Brand tokens captured from obsidian.md CSS: canvas #0F0F0F, surfaces
  #1E1E1E / #262626, text #EEEEEE, muted #A3A3A3, CTA #7C3AED, link #A88BFA,
  type Inter (site font stack), sentence-case headlines with a full stop.
- UI is rebuilt in HTML exactly the way obsidian.md does its own product
  mockups (dark app window, traffic lights, file tree, tabs, status bar).
- Score: original, composed procedurally for this cut (96 BPM, D major,
  12 bars = 30 s); every scene change lands on a bar line.
